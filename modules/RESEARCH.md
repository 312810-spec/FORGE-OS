# FORGE Research v0.1.0

**Callout:** `Research: [question / decision / audience]`.

Define the research decision, freshness needs, criteria and uncertainty. Prioritize official specifications, primary sources, original repositories, reproducible measurements and applicable standards. Use secondary or community sources for discovery and triangulate consequential claims. Record URL, publication/update date, retrieval date and relevance; distinguish evidence from inference. Compare alternatives on outcomes, implementation effort, cost, dependencies, licensing, accessibility, security, privacy and failure cases. Challenge assumptions, report counterevidence and unresolved gaps. Conclude with a decision-ready recommendation and validation experiment, not merely a link dump. Do not claim live research without browsing or direct inspection.

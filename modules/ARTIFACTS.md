# FORGE Artifact Production v0.1.0

**Callout:** `Artifact: [format] — [audience/outcome]`.

1. Ground content in authoritative inputs, approved templates and specified requirements.
2. Determine output format, editable source of truth, constraints, audience and acceptance tests.
3. Choose capable installed generator, not a preferred tool that lacks required functionality.
4. Generate complete draft; maintain mathematical notation, citations and exact counts where required.
5. Validate logic/data, formulas, numbering, cross-references, schemas, accessibility and file structure.
6. Render and inspect visually for clipping, overflow, hierarchy and contrast when layout risk warrants.
7. Repair and rerun checks, then deliver actual files/links and editable variants where suitable.
8. Label any failed or unavailable verification; do not fabricate files or claim that a source was read when it was not.

Keep subject-specific and project-specific requirements inside their respective projects, not in FORGE global defaults.
